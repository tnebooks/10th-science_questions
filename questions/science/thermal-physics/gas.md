The value of universal gas constant:

The universal gas constant ($R$) is a physical constant featured in the ideal gas law ($PV = nRT$). Its value in SI units is approximately $8.314 \text{ J mol}^{-1} \text{ K}^{-1}$. Option (c) is the value for Boltzmann's constant ($1.38 \times 10^{-23} \text{ J/K}$), while 8.31 is the standard value used for the molar gas constant.
