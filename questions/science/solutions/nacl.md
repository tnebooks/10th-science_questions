Solubility of NaCl in 100ml water is 36g. If 25g of salt is dissolved in 100ml of water how much more salt is required for saturation _____________.

---

Amount of solute required for saturation = Solubility value ($36\text{ g}$) - Dissolved amount ($25\text{ g}$) = $36 - 25 = \mathbf{11\text{ g}}$.
